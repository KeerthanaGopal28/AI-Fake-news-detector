# Fake_News_Detector
- **Problem:** There is a critical need for an intelligent and accessible system that can accurately detect and prevent the spread of fake news , ensuring that users receive reliable information. With the increasing volume of online content, misinformation can easily mislead the public , damage reputations, and influence social and political decisions.
- The rapid spread of misinformation and fake news across digital platforms has become a major challenge, influencing public opinion, creating social division, and undermining trust in authentic information sources. Traditional fact-checking methods are often slow, manual, and unable to keep up with the vast volume of online content.
